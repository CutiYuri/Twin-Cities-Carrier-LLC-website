About Twin Cities Carrier

Twin Cities Carrier LLC is a locally owned freight carrier based in Roseville, Minnesota. We've been operating as an FMCSA-authorized interstate common carrier since 2009, hauling general freight and contracted U.S. Mail for shippers across the Twin Cities and the wider Midwest.

Small fleet, hands-on service

We run a small, well-maintained fleet out of Roseville — and we've kept it that way on purpose. Staying small means every load gets personal attention, and every customer can reach the people actually running their freight, not a dispatch queue.

Built on safety and consistency

Over more than fifteen years in business, we've maintained a strong safety record and stayed current on every compliance requirement that comes with interstate authority. That track record is the reason shippers keep bringing us their freight, load after load.

Twin Cities based, Midwest reach

Being based in the Twin Cities means we understand the local shipping landscape — the docks, the routes, the seasonal challenges — while our interstate authority lets us take freight well beyond Minnesota when you need it.

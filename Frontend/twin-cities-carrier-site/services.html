Our Services

Twin Cities Carrier LLC is an FMCSA-authorized interstate common carrier (DOT #1905892, MC #686416) based in Roseville, MN. We run a small, well-maintained fleet, which lets us stay hands-on with every load — from a single pallet to a standing weekly run.

General Freight Hauling

We move general commodities and dry van freight for shippers across the Twin Cities and greater Minnesota. Whether it's a one-time shipment or recurring freight, we work with you to build a pickup and delivery schedule that fits your operation.

U.S. Mail Transport

We've been trusted with contracted U.S. Mail hauling, work that demands consistency and accountability. It's a good reflection of how we handle every load we take on, mail or otherwise.

Regional & Interstate Routes

Based out of Roseville, we cover local Twin Cities deliveries as well as longer regional and interstate runs throughout the Midwest. Our authority and insurance are active and on file, so freight can move across state lines without added paperwork on your end.

Dedicated & Scheduled Runs

For businesses that ship regularly, we can set up a dedicated or recurring schedule so you always know when your freight is picked up and delivered — and who's driving it.

General commodities

Dry van freight of nearly any type, loaded and hauled with care.

Full & partial loads

From single-pallet shipments to full trailer loads, sized to what you actually need moved.

Compliance you can verify

Active FMCSA authority, insurance on file, and a clean, verifiable safety record.
